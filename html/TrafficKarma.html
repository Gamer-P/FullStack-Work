Challenges

Challenges Faced

Managing NPC Traffic

High Complexity

Coordinating dozens of NPC vehicles across multiple intersections without deadlocks or unrealistic behavior required extensive testing.

Solution: Prioritized waypoint queuing with dynamic spacing to prevent pile-ups.

Balancing the Karma System

Design Challenge

Too strict and the game feels punishing. Too lenient and the karma mechanic becomes meaningless. Finding the sweet spot took multiple iterations.

Solution: Weighted point system with gradual consequences instead of instant extremes.

Performance Optimization

Critical

Running physics simulation, NPC AI, traffic lights, and karma calculations simultaneously caused frame drops on lower-end hardware.

Solution: Object pooling for NPCs, simplified collision meshes, and coroutine-based signal timers.
